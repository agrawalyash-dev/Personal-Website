## About

Namaste, I'm Yash Agrawal, a Product Engineer. I love thinking up new products
and ideas that solve real problems, and then using technology to bring them to
life. For me, product, business, and technology aren't separate things. I think
about all three together.

My journey started with chess. I dreamed of becoming a grandmaster and gave two
years of my life to that goal. I didn't make it, but chess taught me patience,
strategy, and how to keep going after a loss. I still play, just for the love of
the game now.

After chess, I explored content creation, design, and trading. None of them
clicked the way I hoped, but each taught me something I still use. Then I found
software development, and it fit who I am: someone who likes to imagine a
product, build it, and watch it come alive.

Today, I'm building Fouroc. My goals keep evolving, but one thing stays constant
in all of them: product thinking. :::

## VUIN

VUIN, short for Virtual Universe from India, is an invite-only social network
for tech professionals, and the first product from Fouroc Softwares.

My focus right now is making VUIN the one place where India's tech community
comes together.

- **Build your network.** Connect with engineers, designers, founders and makers
  who share what you care about.
- **Share your work.** Post what you're building, learning and shipping, and get
  seen by people who understand it.
- **A feed worth reading.** See the work of people in your network, shaped by
  real engagement instead of noise.
- **Give back.** Join conversations, help others grow, and contribute to the
  tech community.

Invites keep the room small enough to be genuine and serious enough to be
useful.

Visit [vuin.in](https://vuin.in). :::

## Building for Bharat, from Bharat

I want to change that. Everything I build starts here, in Vadodara, and is made
